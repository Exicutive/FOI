Our story & how we work.

OUR STORY

Built for people who think long-term.

We founded FOI on the understanding that real estate should not be reduced to a transaction. It is a strategic move, a lifestyle decision, and often a generational one. We bring strategic clarity into every conversation.

Measured rigor, discreet guidance.

From the first brief to final negotiation, our process is tailored to the individual or institution in front of us. We pair local insight with sound advisory judgment to ensure the outcome matches the ambition behind it.
