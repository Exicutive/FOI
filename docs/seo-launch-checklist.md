# SEO launch checklist

## Completed in the site

- Each primary page has its own title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph data, and Twitter card data.
- The home page contains business and website structured data for FOI Developments.
- The sitemap is generated from the live property and development listing pages during every production build.
- The sitemap is declared in `robots.txt` and private source paths are excluded from crawling.
- Contact details and property locations are consistent with the site content.

## Complete after publishing

1. Verify `https://www.foidevs.com` in Google Search Console using a DNS record or HTML verification file.
2. Submit `https://www.foidevs.com/sitemap.xml` in Search Console.
3. Use Google’s Rich Results Test on the home page to check the business structured data.
4. Create or claim the FOI Developments Google Business Profile with the same name, phone number, address, website, and working hours used on the site.
5. Request indexing for the home page, properties page, and each active property page after a material update.

Search visibility also depends on authentic local citations, useful property descriptions, current imagery, and real customer reviews. Do not publish invented reviews, ratings, or unavailable listings.
